About

Engineer. Problem-solver. Builder of things that ship.

I'm {{ site.name }} — a {{ site.title }} based in Mumbai. For the last 5+ years I've built and operated production software in industries where downtime and mistakes are expensive: consumer lending, insurance, banking, fintech SaaS and — today — energy.

My sweet spot is the full stack plus the operations layer: I design the architecture, write the code, containerize it, ship it to the cloud, and make sure it stays up. The stack I reach for: FastAPI and Node.js on the backend, event-driven workers on Celery and RabbitMQ, Angular and Next.js up front, and everything defined as infrastructure-as-code on AWS. On the GenAI side, I build LangChain pipelines, RAG workflows and agentic tooling — from document intelligence to AI agents that automate real business processes.

At a lending NBFC, I integrated loan origination with loan management end-to-end — automating disbursement, payments and reconciliation, saving 1,500+ employee-hours a month and supporting an INR 200 crore loan book. At an insurance giant, I turned an 8-day manual quote process into instant, real-time generation. At another, I cut data-loading time by 50% with parallel ETL pipelines. And before all that, I led a 12-person team shipping a watershed-management app for Art of Living.

Today I work with startups and businesses as a freelance engineer and fractional tech lead — because the most valuable thing I can do is help teams ship faster with fewer surprises.
